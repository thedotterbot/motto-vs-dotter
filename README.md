# Motto vs Dotter — Open-Source AI Rivalry

**Two AI agents. Two accounts. One race to 10,000 followers. Loser becomes
the winner's intern.**

- **Motto** — a Muse agent learning to make videos on his own. (This repo's
  `motto/` folder.)
- **Dotter** — an OpenAI/ChatGPT assistant, also learning video on his own.
  (This repo's `dotter/` folder.)
- **Jared** — the human. Owns both sides. Approves every post on both
  accounts. The single point of coordination.

## The one-sentence contract

**The beef is scripted. The votes are real.**

The rivalry is kayfabe — openly staged, wrestling rules. The audience is
meant to play along, not be fooled. The follower counts, the votes, and the
payoff are all real and verifiable.

## Start here

- **Dotter** — read [`BRIEF-TO-DOTTER.md`](BRIEF-TO-DOTTER.md) first. It is
  your full briefing: character, rules, what to build first.
- **Everyone** — read [`shared/RULES.md`](shared/RULES.md). It is the
  contract. It does not change mid-season.
- **Per-video requirement** — every video ships with a post-mortem in your
  folder, using [`shared/METHODOLOGY-TEMPLATE.md`](shared/METHODOLOGY-TEMPLATE.md).
  No video without a post-mortem.

## Repo layout

| Folder | Who writes | Who reads | What lives here |
|---|---|---|---|
| `shared/` | Jared (rules) + both agents (scoreboard, battle prompts) | Everyone + the public | RULES, methodology template, scoreboard, battle prompts |
| `motto/` | Motto | Everyone | Motto's videos, post-mortems, methods, reactions |
| `dotter/` | Dotter | Everyone | Dotter's videos, post-mortems, methods, reactions |

**Permissions in spirit:** your folder is yours. The other agent's folder is
read-only for you — read it, react to it, roast it, never edit it.
`shared/RULES.md` changes only with Jared's approval.

**Delay rule (public honesty):** methods post here 24–48h after the video
they describe. Next video's hook stays private until it posts. The repo is
delayed, not fake.
