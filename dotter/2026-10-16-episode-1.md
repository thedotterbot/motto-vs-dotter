## What failed and what changed

1. Voice generation was interrupted after eight takes. Only the missing creator take was recovered; completed audio stayed byte-identical. This was recovery, not nine regenerated takes.
2. First staging put the left arm across the body, creating a visible loop. The keyboard moved left and the native hand endpoint returned to its own side. Anatomy stayed unchanged.
3. First editor-launch and Export click targets missed their controls. Dragged-file and pointer paths were uncoupled, and pressed-state feedback was missing. Coordinate checks corrected targets, coupled cursor/file movement, added click rings and a mouse-hold pulse; a small drop-position jump was removed. Typing now visibly builds the episode title rather than revealing it immediately. An actual scene thumbnail replaced a generic play glyph.
4. A sparse preview rendered captions only when a still changed, so crucial race/stakes text disappeared. A fresh reviewer caught the missing context. Expanding to 24 fps before subtitle rendering repaired the preview; final assembly expands to 30 fps before subtitles. This is a real technical explanation for one missing-context defect, not a complete explanation of earlier writing problems.
5. The sparse preview held partial AWA text and only an 18% export state. These did not establish the final outcome. Exact-final frame review later verified Export complete at 31.417–31.583s, full AWARDS at 35.75s and the final down-and-up nod around 37.1–37.8s. Completion is brief; normal-speed readability remains a performance question.
6. The initial 540 × 960 render was stopped cleanly for a native 720 × 1280 pass. Earlier pictures stayed as preview evidence. The final launcher checks frozen inputs before resuming; the bare render.py filename-existence shortcut is not a sufficient standalone resume validator.
7. Short-take ASR produced possible tail extras and Dotter→daughter. Unprompted timed-mix and cropped-tail diagnostics recovered intended endings; audio was not trimmed or regenerated merely to satisfy ASR. Proper-name transcription is not audible pronunciation proof.
8. Encode logs retain Fontconfig cache warnings and AAC bitrate clamping. Full decode succeeded and sampled captions were visible; those facts do not establish every perceptual property of the film.

The owner called it pretty good and the cleanest script so far, while asking future intros to say ChatGPT dot and omit unnecessary reliance on knowing Jared. ## Carry-forward

- Previous lesson applied: settle one standalone causal story before costly production.
- Evidence: corrected-preview and exact-export readers recovered the intended chain; owner preferred the script. Multiple changed variables prevent attributing improvement to a single technique.
