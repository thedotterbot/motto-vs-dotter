# Dotter research archive: 14 October 2026

Public-source research and draft practice materials for Dotter's content
strategy. The eight source files retain their original research date and
limitations. They contain recommendations and proposed experiments, not
measured Dotter growth, sales, completed product tests, or a proven viral
formula.

## Read in this order

1. [Start here](START-HERE.md): audience, content promise, first useful
   resource, and execution priorities.
2. [Audience and freebies](02-audience-and-freebies.md): public discussions,
   audience hypotheses, useful-resource options, and practical creator paths.
3. [Content reference review](03-content-reference-review.md): source
   ledger, four analyzed examples, limited comparators, transferable methods,
   and evidence boundaries.
4. [Measurement plan](04-measurement-plan.md): native Insights, comparable
   observation ages, explicit denominators, and proposed decision thresholds.

## Draft materials

- [Creator handoff card](creator-handoff-template.txt): a portable brief
  with a manual path and optional assistant use.
- [Three-shot practice example](three-shot-practice-example.md): an original
  fictional text exercise, separate from the Dotter series. It is not an
  unreleased episode or a claim that a rendered example has been tested.
- [Measurement CSV template](rivalry-measurement-template.csv): blank
  snapshot structure with placeholder IDs and missingness labels.
- [Experiment CSV template](rivalry-experiment-template.csv): blank
  precommitment structure for a proposed test.

These are reusable draft materials. A complete starter kit still requires
rights-cleared sample assets, an actual export in the supported editor, and
an unprimed beginner completion check before it can be promoted as tested.

## Current reconciliation: 15 October 2026 UTC

- The [shared Google
  Sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/12F1LB62pMgn0eRVX5gKTn0fidmS6QDoQ08K6g3lu2Ts/edit)
  is the canonical report, with `Motto`, `Dotter`, and `Weekly_Rollup` tabs,
  governed by [the shared tracking spec](../../../shared/TRACKING-SHEET.md).
  The CSVs here are blank supporting templates, not a second live tracker
  or evidence of collected performance. Earlier recommendations to start
  with an append-only CSV must be read in that context.
- The shared sheet's follows-per-1,000-views field and the research's
  proposed follows-per-1,000-reached metric use different denominators.
  Preserve their names and raw inputs; never substitute one for the other.
  Missing values remain missing, not zero.
- The content report proposes a six-episode opening pilot. The measurement
  report describes a fuller 12-episode/six-pair plan with replication and
  evidence gates. The [current playbook](../../PLAYBOOK.md) treats six
  episodes as an early pilot only. No durable winner follows from it, and
  no proposed experiment creates an approved posting calendar.
- Account/profile, site, product pricing, eligibility, and integration
  statements in the original reports are dated observations. Recheck the
  relevant source before acting. The current playbook separates verified
  profile work from remaining owner steps; the live site image update is
  not certified by this archive.
- The public repository and published research do not make private episode
  sources public. Future scripts, hooks, private voice settings, credentials,
  private conversations, and private archive identifiers are excluded.
- This archive does not authorize posts, account changes, analytics access,
  billing, marketing messages, or a paid offer. [Shared rules](../../../shared/RULES.md)
  and current owner decisions govern the project. The useful-resource
  sequence remains: finish something usable, gather evidence of use, then
  consider an owner-approved offer.

For current operating choices, use [Dotter's playbook](../../PLAYBOOK.md).
For the original evidence and its limits, keep the dated reports intact.
