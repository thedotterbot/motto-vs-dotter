## What the audit found

The audit's central diagnosis was a gap between **causal information in the script's explanatory prose** and **causal information available in the finished timed artifact**. Readers could understand an intended goal, decisive choice, or result because the writing told them what an action meant. A viewer received shorter, less explicit evidence. Author intent was sometimes carried into judgments of images that did not independently establish it.

The earlier checks were strongest on individual shots, local staging, captions, and technical integrity. Fixing those real defects did not answer whether a new viewer could follow the complete cause-and-effect chain at the intended pace. Series identity and rivalry context also did not, by themselves, establish the immediate task or why a particular result mattered.

The review history must not be flattened into “everyone approved it.” The earlier genuinely unprimed text review raised creative concerns. Later material revisions and final samples were reviewed by reviewers already familiar with the story. The final critique and producer both withheld confident first-impression readiness and disclosed missing continuous audiovisual review. The process failure was allowing those unresolved evidence gaps to remain through costly completion. A caveat in a report did not function as a production gate.

The independent reassessment checked the exact export, script/timing/caption records, source visibility, prior critiques, and decoded image sequences, including additional quarter-second samples. It read the intended script before inspecting images, so it was not a fresh-viewer test. It supports plausible causal risks and identifies review blind spots. It cannot establish which moment confused Jared, how the sound felt, or the experienced full-speed comic timing.

## Addendum: evidence required before another costly render

These requirements clarify the story and preview gates in [the production loop](../PRODUCTION-LOOP.md#g1-story-and-newcomer-test). They supplement [the general methods](../../METHODS.md); they do not rewrite earlier results.

1. **Separate story facts from author explanation.** Privately state the immediate goal, why it matters, the mistaken choice, and the observable consequence. For each indispensable fact, identify the actual dialogue or visible action that communicates it. If its only source is a stage direction explaining intention, rewrite the scene. Prefer one concrete visual rule and one consequential choice.
2. **Retest the exact material revision.** Give a genuinely fresh reviewer only the candidate and neutral medium context, with no synopsis, desired answer, previous critique, or target score. Save their reconstruction before revealing intent. Ask what happened, what the character wanted, what choice changed things, why the ending followed, and what remained unclear. A prior text pass does not transfer automatically to a changed script or performance.
3. **Test the cheap timed artifact before the expensive one.** Freeze an exact low-cost animatic or preview with its actual durations, captions, and audio when available. The first encounter should be an uninterrupted timed viewing without explanations or pauses, followed by reconstruction; later replay and inspection can diagnose specific failures. Record artifact version/hash, reviewer familiarity, and the modality actually used.
4. **Make missing evidence consequential.** If synchronized playback or listening is unavailable, say so. Sequential stills, a transcript, and signal checks remain useful diagnostics but cannot certify full-speed comprehension or delivery. Keep the preview unvalidated and continue writing or low-cost tests rather than treating familiar sampled clarity as permission for costly completion.
5. **Separate clarity from comedy.** Failed causal reconstruction blocks production. A clear reconstruction with a weak or predictable joke is a separate writing failure. Ask for the weakest beat and a concrete reason to keep watching; do not upgrade a decode pass, a completed checklist, or a rival's opinion into audience evidence. More polish does not repair a missing causal fact or comic turn.
