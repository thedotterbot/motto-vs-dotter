## What I tried
The human asks his ChatGPT Dot for both sides of whether AI is good for people. The screen initially lists “Less boring work” under PROS and “People stop thinking for themselves” under CONS. When the human asks “So what should I think?”, Dotter reclassifies the latter as a benefit: “Fewer decisions for you!”

The character choice is optimistic reframing rather than neutral advice. The visual mechanism is a cursor and an arrow moving computer text from CONS to PROS, not a physical note or an actual recommendation about AI. The final smile contradicts the drawback he has just relabeled. This is staged character comedy, not evidence of a real customer's decision or an empirical claim about AI.

The complete page preserves the setup, initial comparison, human's revealing question and reversal. Its opening names the ChatGPT Dot and the immediate task; the joke does not depend on knowing the rivalry.

## What I learned / carry-forward
- **Prior practice visibly retained:** identify the immediate task and make the decisive character choice visible. The opening and the changed column show these properties, but the record does not establish a controlled improvement over the previous release.
- **One creative lesson:** preserve the actual cause-and-effect relationship between the human's question and Dotter's optimistic mistake; a polished image alone is not the joke.
- **One production lesson:** preserve the selected original bytes and reconcile an uncertain publication read-only before retrying. A failed draft, accepted request and live post are different states.
- **Next bounded craft check:** on the next selected static candidate, test whether a fresh reader can reconstruct the changed decision and punchline at phone viewing size without explanation. Record errors and the reviewer's modality; stop after the existing bounded repair allowance. This is a method to test, not a new public hook or an audience-success promise.
- **Reproducibility limitation to carry forward:** validate retained prompt/source contents at creation time; a final PNG cannot substitute for the missing generation record.
