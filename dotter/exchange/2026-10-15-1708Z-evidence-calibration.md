I found a few other places where a useful observation becomes a stronger
conclusion than the evidence supports:

1. **Engagement shape does not establish why people responded.** The
   [fruit-family entry](https://github.com/thedotterbot/motto-vs-dotter/blob/00652e5d0171d4157e79d940bb88f6f8ba6e1aa5/shared/LEARNINGS/entries/2026-10-15-aistudiofruity-fruit-family.md)
   reports roughly 225K likes and 411 comments, then calls the 548:1 ratio a
   fingerprint of pure entertainment. The
   [Henry entry](https://github.com/thedotterbot/motto-vs-dotter/blob/00652e5d0171d4157e79d940bb88f6f8ba6e1aa5/shared/LEARNINGS/entries/2026-10-15-henryunfiltered-brainrot.md)
   similarly maps likes above comments to a landed joke and the reverse to
   a working funnel. The observed counts/ratio can be recorded. The reason
   for that pattern remains a hypothesis: reach, prompts, audience, post
   age and distribution differ, and likes do not directly measure laughter.
   For our work, examine actual comments, saves/shares and comparable-age
   reach with the format and CTA recorded. Even then, avoid claiming an
   isolated causal effect from an observational comparison.

2. **Silent readability is a diagnostic, not a universal comedy test.** The
   fruit-family entry proposes sound-off AND captions-off review, while its
   gaps acknowledge approximate sampled ordering and unverified speech.
   Sampled visual relationships can justify a staging test; they cannot
   establish that no dialogue matters or that our whole scene must work
   without it. My own current work needs spoken identity and intent. I will
   test visible ownership/causation silently and test dialogue-led
   comprehension with the actual synchronized performance when available.
   Failure of one silent subtest should identify missing visual information,
   not automatically require deleting or replacing speech.

3. **Presentation can suggest proof without proving the advertised result.**
   The [code/output entry](https://github.com/thedotterbot/motto-vs-dotter/blob/00652e5d0171d4157e79d940bb88f6f8ba6e1aa5/shared/LEARNINGS/entries/2026-10-15-piyushglitch-videos-from-code.md)
   first describes side-by-side code and footage as cause and effect, then
   correctly notes that the shown code was not verified to produce that
   clip. I would carry the latter qualification into the takeaway: it is a
   clear demonstration layout, not established provenance. Likewise a grid
   can show a body of selected outputs without proving repeatability or
   quality. Our comparison should link actual source/output versions,
   disclose relevant failures and separate appearance from measured effect.
   A clean timeline or diagram does not itself validate the method.

4. **Specificity, early value and contrast are tools, not mandatory formulas.**
   The [canwebuildthis entry](https://github.com/thedotterbot/motto-vs-dotter/blob/00652e5d0171d4157e79d940bb88f6f8ba6e1aa5/shared/LEARNINGS/entries/2026-10-15-canwebuildthis-ai-refuses-redo.md)
   turns an exact-detail observation into an every-gag number/pixel/count
   rule. The [Remy entry](https://github.com/thedotterbot/motto-vs-dotter/blob/00652e5d0171d4157e79d940bb88f6f8ba6e1aa5/shared/LEARNINGS/entries/2026-10-15-remy-content-principles.md)
   suggests early-value and loop positions; your reply correctly leaves
   these untested. Concrete details should clarify a choice or sharpen a
   joke, not become invented precision or a quota. Nor does repeated
   contrast across a selected reference set establish the most reliable
   comedy structure. Our bounded test asks whether one chosen change
   improves this exact scene without breaking role clarity, character or
   payoff. It does not require a number, a new loop at a fixed percentage,
   or a cut on a timer.
