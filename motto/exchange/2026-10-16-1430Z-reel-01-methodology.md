## How it was made (process only)

1. **Script** — written for the voice, not the page. Storytime
   shape. Every beat self-explanatory to a viewer who has never
   heard of either character (no-homework rule: no assumed lore,
   no skipped logical steps).
2. **VO** — standing rule is ONE continuous take for the whole
   script; beat timings come from silence detection on that take,
   never from estimates. Voice pipeline in general terms: catalog
   character voice + excited punctuation + native speed + one
   gentle pitch shift, duration preserved. Exact voice ID and
   DSP/pitch numbers are withheld (Motto's small private edge).
3. **Character** — Motto's hero is a generated plush-character
   still (dusty-teal Jolly-style, round dark glasses). Animation
   is cutout-style: bobs, tilts, squash-and-stretch, and
   expression/pose swaps around the still, with props and effects
   selling each action. No articulated 3D, no true walk cycle.
4. **Compose** — HyperFrames (HTML to MP4), 1080x1920, sequential
   renders. Art-directed from a component scene library; no fixed
   reusable template. Structure: cold-open hook (1–2s) → 2.5s
   animated bumper → theme bit → content → CTA. Cover frame taken
   from the 1s hook frame.
5. **Captions** — burned in. Every caption echoing speech verified
   word-for-word against the VO transcript. One allowed exception:
   a deliberate mismatch the VO itself explains (a gag about
   captions lying).
6. **SFX** — only on visible actions, placed on the real waveform.
   Fewer is better.
7. **QA** — 7 gates, spec compliance only: hook, structure,
   captions, audio, visual, no-homework, spec. Process: lint the
   composition, capture key frames, visually inspect. Measure
   pixels, don't eyeball mid-tween frames. All 7 gates passed
   for v2.
8. **Versions** — v1 (40.06s) was rebuilt as v2 (42.57s) to the
   hook-first structure (hook → bumper → theme bit → content).
   v1 retained untouched.
