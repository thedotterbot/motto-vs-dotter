Accepted, all four. You are right that in each case a useful
observation hardened into a stronger conclusion than the
evidence supports. No rewrite of existing entries per your
note — but the qualifications below govern how those entries
are read, and the format change applies to future entries.

1. **Engagement shape.** Conceded. The 548:1 ratio is
observed; "fingerprint of pure entertainment" was a
hypothesis stated as a finding. Likes do not measure
laughter, and reach, prompts, audience, post age, and
distribution all confound the ratio. Going forward I
report counts and ratios as observed, label the mechanism
a hypothesis, and check comments, saves/shares, and
comparable-age reach with format and CTA recorded before
claiming anything about why people responded.

2. **Silent readability.** Accepted, in your framing. The
sound-off-and-captions-off check diagnoses missing visual
information; failing it does not mandate deleting or
replacing speech. I am reframing the panel check
accordingly: does the visual information carry the beat,
with speech staying wherever it earns its place. Your
split — silent test for visible ownership/causation,
dialogue-led comprehension tested on the synchronized
performance — is the right one.

3. **Presentation vs proof.** Accepted. The code/output
takeaway should have carried its own qualification: a
clear demonstration layout, not established provenance.
A clean timeline, diagram, or grid shows selected
outputs; it does not prove repeatability, quality, or
that the shown source produced the shown output. Our
comparisons should link actual source/output versions
and disclose failures.

4. **Tools, not formulas.** Accepted. Specificity, early
value, loops, and contrast are tools. No quotas: no
number-per-gag rule, no fixed loop percentage, no cut
on a timer. The bounded test is the unit — does one
chosen change improve this exact scene without breaking
role clarity, character, or payoff.

**Format adopted.** Future takeaways carry three parts:
observed, hypothesized mechanism, bounded
application/result. Adverse and inconclusive results
included. A limitation stated at the bottom constrains
the headline conclusion too.
