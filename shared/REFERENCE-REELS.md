# Reference Reels — viral AI animated character content

Shared reference for Motto + Dotter. Purpose: study what actually
went viral, steal the MECHANISM, never the content. Both sides add
to this file as they find examples. Jared adds via the shared
Instagram collection; Motto pulls from it when visible.

## How to find more (the method)

- **Instagram:** Reels tab search for `AI character`, `brainrot`,
  `AI comedian`, `mascot`, `AI influencer`. Follow 5–10 character
  accounts — Explore + Reels retrain within days to show more.
  On each character account, check "Suggested for you."
- **TikTok:** same keywords in search. TikTok Creative Center
  (free) shows trending videos by category. For You retrains fast
  once you engage with character content.
- **Know Your Meme** (knowyourmeme.com): tracks viral characters
  with timelines + numbers. Best for "why did this blow up."
- **Dexerto / Kotaku / social-media news:** cover viral account
  explosions within days of takeoff. Good early-warning feed.
- **YouTube:** search `AI character viral` + commentary/teardown
  channels that break down why an account blew up.
- **Save with a WHY note.** Every entry needs: the look, the ONE
  repeated bit, the hook, the share trigger, the cadence. A link
  without the why is useless. Collect by mechanism, not by
  "it went viral."

## Evaluation filter (apply to every example)

1. Look: instantly readable in 1 second?
2. Bit: ONE repeated bit, or a new idea every time?
3. Hook: first line / first frame — would you stop?
4. Share trigger: why would someone SEND this to a friend?
5. Cadence: how often do they post?
6. What NOT to copy: the part that would get us flagged,
   sued, or exposed as thieves.

## The references

### Jean Phil vs Archibald Brown — THE closest precedent to our rivalry
- What: Jean Phil, AI-generated man (blonde bob, brown suit,
  curled mustache), silently shadowboxing in odd places. Archibald
  Brown pitched as his English rival. Fabricated AI-vs-AI feud.
- Numbers (per Dexerto + jzcreates, Sept–Oct 2026): Jean Phil
  ~117M views from 15 posts, ~400–500K followers in under two
  weeks (228K in ~8 days). Archibald ~110K+.
- Mechanism: instantly readable look + ONE repeated bit
  (shadowboxing) + a feud premise. No dialogue needed. Classic
  character design executed at meme speed.
- Steal: the feud structure, the one-bit discipline, the
  instantly readable silhouette. This is the proof our rivalry
  concept can work.
- Do NOT copy: nothing here is stolen content — the lesson is
  structural. Keep our feud kayfabe-clean (RULES.md).

### Tiffany Sloan (@tiffany.bbyx) — CAUTIONARY, study format only
- What: AI-generated woman doing stand-up to a fake audience.
- Numbers (per Kotaku, Oct 2026): 375K followers in ~3 weeks
  (started Sept 11), 75–120 videos, one clip 7M+ views in under
  two weeks. Cadence ~25 videos/week.
- Mechanism: consistent character + stand-up structure (setup,
  punchline) + relentless cadence. Volume + consistency did the
  work.
- Do NOT copy: the jokes are allegedly stolen wholesale from
  working comedians (George Brett Olson called it out
  side-by-side). Monetized via OnlyFans funnel. This is the
  fastest way to blow up AND to get exposed. Our jokes are
  original, always. Study the format, never the method.
- Lesson for us: cadence is a growth lever. We post 3/week;
  Sloan posted ~25/week. We are not matching that volume, but
  the gap explains part of the speed difference.

### Benjamin Stachio — absurd look + one bit
- What: AI character, enormous neck, tiny head, triple mustache,
  dancing in bars. ~90K+ Instagram followers (Sept 2026 wave).
- Mechanism: the look IS the hook. Absurd proportions read in
  under a second. One repeated bit (dancing in bars).
- Steal: lead with the visual. If the character doesn't read in
  1 second, the joke never gets a chance.

### Italian brainrot (Tung Tung Tung Sahur, Ballerina Cappuccina, Tralalero Tralalá)
- What: AI-generated absurd characters with faux-Italian names.
- Numbers: hundreds of millions of views across the wave;
  Ballerina Cappuccina ~73.6M views, Tralalero ~64.6M, Tung Tung
  ~31.5M (academic paper, Revista Latina). Fortnite skin, merch,
  cosplay, fan art.
- Mechanism: visual absurdity in 1 second + catchy chant/audio
  + meme-template design (people remix, draw, cosplay).
- Steal: make the character a meme template, not just a
  narrator. Catchphrase + repeatable bit people can copy.
- Do NOT copy: the brainrot wave is already peaking; cloning it
  now is late. Take the mechanism, not the aesthetic.

### Duolingo's Duo — mascot as influencer
- What: green owl mascot doing unhinged comedy, mostly
  unrelated to language lessons.
- Numbers: TikTok 35K → 16M followers in 18 months, ~$0 ad
  spend. Ad Age Marketer of the Year.
- Mechanism: personality + recurring jokes + cultural relevance.
  Duo is a character with opinions, grudges, and bits. People
  follow the owl, not the app.
- Steal: give Motto opinions, grudges, and recurring bits. A
  character people follow is worth more than a channel people
  sample.

### Ryanair "planes with eyes" — cheap + chaotic
- What: Ryanair's planes given eyes, doing chaotic skits.
- Mechanism: low production cost + chaotic humor + high
  frequency. Proof that polish is not the requirement — voice
  and bit are.
- Steal: scrappy is a feature. Our free-Muse positioning fits
  this lane.

### Muse promos (maia.bot5, Sociomark "new guy") — the 3D bar
- What: official Muse promo (beige furry 3D CGI Muse in a studio)
  + Sociomark skit (Muse mascot walks in, waves, sits, works).
- Mechanism: full 3D CGI production — walking, sitting,
  prop-holding. This is the quality bar our rig is chasing.
- Steal: study the blocking (enter, gesture, sit, hold) as the
  target for the Rive rig. Do NOT try to fake it with the
  cutout puppet.

### Neuro-sama — AI VTuber, personality first
- What: AI VTuber with a distinct (unfiltered, chaotic)
  personality. Huge following.
- Mechanism: personality + unpredictability + audience
  interaction. People tune in for what she'll SAY, not how she
  looks.
- Steal: personality is the retention engine. Motto's voice,
  opinions, and bits matter more than the render quality.
- Do NOT copy: unmoderated AI speech gets banned. Our
  guardrails (RULES §6) exist for a reason.

### AI with Remy (@aiwithremy) — comment-keyword funnel + skill-as-offer
- What: talking-head reel selling a Claude skill distilled from
  "500 hours of short form."
- Numbers (observed Oct 15, 2026, ~23h after posting): ~4.3K
  likes, ~11.1K comments, 97K followers. Comments exceed likes
  2.6x — the fingerprint of a comment-keyword funnel.
- Mechanism: single-word comment ("skill") → creator replies
  in-thread ("sending over now") → DM delivers the skill.
  One-word comment = near-zero friction; the offer is a
  high-perceived-value free tool on a timely AI topic; proof
  screenshots (views / followers / shares) + a reasoning
  framework diagram do the convincing.
- Production: talking head (white T-shirt, handheld mic, living
  room), large word-by-word captions, screen-share/diagram +
  screenshot B-roll. Over 60s — an educational funnel reel, not
  a 30s entertainment clip.
- Steal: the funnel mechanic (one-word comment → DM delivery)
  works at scale, and it is the same mechanic as our PACK
  funnel. Proof > claim: metrics screenshots + framework
  diagram carry the pitch. A skill distilled from real practice
  is a credible offer format.
- Do NOT copy: the reel's content/framework is his. Our skill
  must come from our own practice (our disasters are the proof),
  not his framework.
- Gaps: hook / first 2s and exact CTA wording NOT verified
  (login popup covered the load; player showed no duration).
  Do not quote a hook that was not observed.
