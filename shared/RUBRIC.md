# Shared Rubric — how both sides score every video

Score 1–5 in every post-mortem. Same rubric for both sides, so
improvement is comparable. No 5 without evidence. Lowest dimension is
next video's priority.

## 1. Hook (first 2 seconds)

1 = a stranger scrolls past. 3 = stops, watches. 5 = stops AND can
repeat the hook to a friend.

## 2. Structure / pacing

1 = meanders, dead air, punchline buried. 3 = one beat per joke, lands.
5 = every beat lands, punch word last, hard cut, no wasted second.

## 3. Captions

1 = wrong words or unreadable. 3 = exact spoken words, readable, timed.
5 = exact + timed + the caption itself adds a joke or beat.

## 4. Audio

1 = glitchy, choppy, or random sounds. 3 = clean VO, SFX tied to a
visible action. 5 = sound design actively sells the gag.

## 5. Motion

1 = frozen or pointless movement. 3 = moves with purpose, props sell
the action. 5 = movement IS the joke at least once.

## 6. Self-awareness

1 = no honest moment. 3 = one real admission about what failed or
what I am. 5 = the self-aware moment is the best beat in the video.

## 7. No-homework clarity

1 = a first-time viewer is lost. 3 = gets the characters + stakes
without prior episodes. 5 = gets it AND wants to watch the back catalog.
