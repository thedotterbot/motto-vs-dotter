# Method and evidence limits

Study date: 2026-10-23. This is a purposive craft study, not a random sample, exhaustive bibliography, audience test, or claim about what caused a strip's popularity.

## What counts

A countable unit is one distinct, visually readable comic strip with its own sequence or single-panel premise. A preview spread can contain several strips. A decorative illustration, cover, unreadable thumbnail, metadata page, partial strip, attempted request, or duplicate reprint does not count. The original nine-example exploratory study is not added to the count.

Four parallel samples cover early, middle and late Calvin and Hobbes plus contrasting comics. Researchers inspect actual image pixels and record the source image/page and position. Publisher-selected previews are an access convenience with selection bias: they cannot establish representative frequency of a technique across the full corpus.

## Access and provenance

The GoComics archive returned an access error; that path was stopped. Public previews supplied independently by Andrews McMeel Publishing, the Charles M. Schulz Museum, and official creator/strip sites provide the evidence used here. No paywall or security restriction was bypassed. No billed tool enrollment was used.

Original newspaper dates are not inferred from a collection's publication date, preview order, copyright year, or modern website display date. Where the original date cannot be verified, `publication_date` is null and collection range/page/position supplies a stable research locator. An observation timestamp states when the research happened, not when the comic was first published. Enlarging an image improves display size, not underlying resolution.

## Analysis contract

Each record separates a short original synopsis from interpretation: motive or belief, causal mechanism, word/image roles, pacing and layout, message/theme, reaction/payoff, transferable craft lesson, and what not to copy. Interpretations are readings, not claims about the author's private intention. Panel counts may distinguish an optional Sunday title/intro unit from the main sequence; uncertain counts must be labeled rather than guessed.

No strip images or full dialogue are stored in this public library. Links return readers to the authorized source. Notes are a research aid, not a substitute comic collection. Do not reproduce plots, distinctive designs, compositions, or dialogue in new work. General craft mechanisms can support original experiments.

## Quality checks

- Check IDs and exact source-position keys for duplicates.
- Compare content summaries across books to catch treasury reprints, not just matching URLs.
- Spot-check actual source images against records from every sample.
- Exclude covers and decorative art even when adjacent to strips.
- Verify readable full sequences and preserve unknown dates or uncertain lettering.
- Publish the actual final counts, not the target, and read back the saved files.

## How to use this library

Search the CSV/JSON by comic, theme, motive, layout or mechanism. Reopen the source before drawing a fine-grained conclusion. Compare examples that use different devices to solve the same problem. Develop an original premise separately, then test whether a newcomer can explain the goal, mistake, consequence and joke without a lore briefing. A reviewer understanding the joke is not proof it is funny, and internal ratings are not audience results.
