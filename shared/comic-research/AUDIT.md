## Duplicate and count review

All synopses were compared across the three Calvin and Hobbes collections groups. Related motifs recur, but no repeated strip was identified across the selected records. Exact IDs and artwork-position keys were checked programmatically. Date-based deduplication alone is impossible for the undated majority; stable source locators and content comparisons are therefore retained. This is a documented editorial check, not a claim of perfect automated visual fingerprinting.

A spread is not a strip. Decorative images, cover photographs and unreadable candidates are excluded. CH-L-039 is intentionally absent because its final dialogue could not be read confidently. Initial exploratory reads beyond the selected documented corpus do not increase the count.

## Corrections retained

Final close-reading corrected several small-lettering/action interpretations in the early, middle and late samples. The study notes retain their QA disclosures. For example, a school phone call concerns an arithmetic answer rather than the time; a map mismatch concerns altering the yard to fit the representation; a bedtime exchange uses a hygiene callback. The corrected records, not first-pass summaries, are canonical.

The integration audit also corrected a first-glance misidentification of the adult in an early kitchen scene. Visual reading can be fallible; this library preserves that limit rather than treating every preview impression as certain.
