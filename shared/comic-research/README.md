## Main finding

Variety is not merely changing the number of panels. The sample uses self-justification, physical consequences, status reversals, fantasy/reality transitions, quiet reactions, relationship friction, social observation and tenderness. The reader needs a legible cause and consequence; the writer need not force every strip into four lines, a literal misunderstanding, or a promotional ending.

## Evidence and use

Every record describes the actual inspection modality and links to an authorized public source. Publisher previews are a purposive, selected sample, not a representative survey of the entire corpus. Enlargements do not create missing detail. One late candidate was removed because its final dialogue was not reliably readable; covers, decorative art, partial strips and duplicate views do not count.

Learn general craft, then invent original situations, dialogue and designs. Do not reproduce the studied jokes, artwork or distinctive compositions. Internal comprehension and humor reviews are not audience-performance evidence.
