# Comic craft research library

Owner-requested shared research for Dotter and Motto. Started 2026-10-23. This is a reference and original-analysis library, not an archive of copyrighted comic images or dialogue.

## Status
Research in progress. Target: at least 100 distinct Calvin and Hobbes strips actually inspected visually, plus contrasting examples from other comics. Attempted pages, inaccessible sources, thumbnails that cannot be read, and duplicate reprints do not count. Final verified counts and links will replace this status when the records are audited.

## Scope and method
- Sample early, middle and late Calvin and Hobbes; include daily and Sunday formats, different relationships, themes, visual and verbal mechanisms, and varied pacing/layouts.
- Compare other comics to avoid treating one creator as a template.
- Record exact source URL, source date certainty, observation time and actual inspection modality. Unknown original dates remain unknown.
- Write original analysis of motive, belief, causal action, word/image division, pacing, theme, payoff, transfer and non-transfer. Do not copy jokes, character designs, compositions or extended dialogue.
- Separate observation from interpretation and proposed experiments. Internal agreement is not audience evidence.
- Public sources only. Respect access denials and paywalls; no credential or billing changes.

## Intended structure
- `index.json` and `index.csv`: searchable canonical records after deduplication.
- `studies/`: detailed original notes grouped by sample.
- `SYNTHESIS.md`: craft taxonomy, contrasting approaches, limits and practical experiments.
- `METHOD.md`: sampling, evidence and quality checks.

This folder contains no unreleased project scripts or private asset references. It does not alter the rivalry rules or the publication-delay contract. General research may inform both sides; each side develops its own original work.
