## What works and why (the content, not the funnel)

- **Skill-count specificity.** 11 skills — a concrete
  bundle size. A number makes a bundle feel substantial
  and checkable.
- **Skill tour as montage.** One animated card per skill,
  fast cuts. Visual variety carries a list that would
  drag as narration.
- **Lead with the strongest skill.** The virality skill
  is presented as the standout — hierarchy, not sequence.
  Order by strength, not by the order the skills were
  built in.
- **Title breakdown as teaching.** Dissect one real
  example into title/hook/structure parts. Teaching by
  dissection — same deconstruction mechanism as the
  branding entry, applied to a title instead of a clip.
- **3-step setup with a timer.** A 10-second timer makes
  setup feel instant and bounded. A timer is a
  credibility device: it turns "easy setup" into a
  measurable claim.
- **Burned-in captions synced to narration.**
  Muted-readable. Same caption discipline as the rest of
  the strong entries.
- **"Rebuild in your own voice" — third occurrence.**
  Opusjake said it, this reel says it. The honest
  creators keep landing on the same line: study others,
  rewrite in your own voice. That is the consensus
  version of the rule our learnings area enforces.

## What does NOT transfer to us

- **Proof: none — mock UI presented as product.**
  Animated mock-UI cards and diagrams only. No real
  dashboard, no code, no screenshot of real results, no
  before/after, no testimonials. The view-count label
  is part of the animation, not a confirmed dashboard.
  Mock UI that looks like a working product is the most
  misleading proof form in this set — it shows a
  product that may not exist in that form.
- **"Claude runs a channel" overstates agency.** Claude
  does not run a channel. A human approves posts — our
  whole approval flow (panel, QA, gate-8, owner verdict)
  exists because autonomy without gates ships bad reels.
  The claim erases the human. Our positioning is
  autonomy WITH gates, never autonomy without humans.
- **10-second setup is marketing.** Paste-a-link setup
  in 10 seconds ignores auth, permissions, testing, and
  learning curve. Our install must be honestly timed
  and tested before promising anything. Never "10
  seconds" unless timed.
- **The 11 skills are HIS product.** We do not copy his
  skill list. Our pack is 3–5 skills + guide + examples.
  Bigger bundle is not better — curation is.
- **Sampling missed some skill cards.** Do not present
  his system as fully observed.
- **No joke, no comedy.** Pure promotional explainer.
  Our reels need the joke too.

## What we apply

1. **Title breakdown as a teaching device.** Dissect one
   of OUR real titles (reel 01/02/04) into title/hook/
   structure parts in a future teaching reel. Real
   titles, real dissection.
2. **Lead with the strongest skill.** When presenting
   the pack, order by strength, not by build sequence.
   Strongest first.
3. **3-step setup with HONEST timing.** Our pack install
   gets a 3-step setup (paste SKILL.md → tell Muse to
   remember it → test with one prompt) with a REALISTIC
   time, tested before promising. Never "10 seconds"
   unless timed.
4. **Real UI only.** Our demos show REAL UI (real
   HyperFrames composition, real QA reports), never mock
   UI presented as product.
5. **Autonomy honesty.** When we describe what Muse
   does, name the human gates (panel, QA, gate-8, owner
   verdict). Autonomy with gates, not autonomy without
   humans.
