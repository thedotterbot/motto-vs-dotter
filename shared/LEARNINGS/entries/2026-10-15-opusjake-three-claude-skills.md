## What works and why (the content, not the funnel)

- **Curation as value.** "Tested many, kept three."
  The creator did the sifting so the viewer doesn't have
  to. Selection IS the product. The pitch is not "here
  are skills" — it is "I threw away the bad ones."
- **Bundle with an arc.** Edit → grow → build is a
  complete pipeline with a beginning, middle, and end —
  not three random tips. A bundle that follows a
  workflow feels complete; a bundle of tips feels like
  a grab bag.
- **One visual proof per claim.** Each skill gets a
  concrete demo: an edited reel, a ranking table, a mock
  store page. One output per claim. The viewer never has
  to take a skill on faith alone.
- **Consistent visual identity.** Playful pixel-art icons
  and graphics throughout. Distinct, memorable, and the
  opposite of generic AI-slop gradients. Identity makes
  a list feel like a product.
- **Bold captions restate each point.** Redundancy for
  muted viewers — the captions carry the argument, not
  just the words.
- **"Rewrite in your own voice" is the honest version.**
  The virality skill is framed as finding what works and
  rewriting it in the creator's own voice. That is the
  line between studying and stealing — the same line our
  learnings area draws (steal the mechanism, never the
  material). Contrast the Tiffany Sloan entry: jokes
  copied wholesale is the do-not-copy version of this.

## What does NOT transfer to us

- **Proof is mockups only.** Skill file, ranking table,
  mock store page — no verified analytics, no
  before/after metrics, no testimonials. The store page
  is illustrative, not a confirmed sale. Same proof gap
  as most of this set: demos shown, outcomes not measured.
- **"Tested every skill" is claimed, not shown.** The
  testing happened off-camera. The reel shows the three
  survivors, never the rejects. Curation claims need the
  rejects to be checkable — otherwise "tested every" is
  just a hook.
- **The three skills are HIS product.** We do not copy
  his skills. Our pack is our own pipeline (idea → script
  → voice → QA), built from our real production.
- **Talking-head delivery is not our format.** Our bet
  is character-led. The explainer energy works here; it
  does not transfer to a plush puppet's delivery.
- **Comment CTA close is funnel.** Noted, not analyzed —
  the lesson is the curation mechanism, not the funnel.
  (Context only: comments exceed likes 509 vs. 210, the
  comment-keyword fingerprint.)

## What we apply

1. **Curation-as-value framing for our pack.** "We
   tested X across real production, kept what survived"
   is a pitch shape. Our pack pitch leads with what we
   threw away, not just what we kept.
2. **Bundle with an arc.** When we present the pack,
   order the skills as a pipeline (idea → script → voice
   → QA), a beginning/middle/end — not a random list.
3. **One visual proof per claim.** Every pack claim gets
   one screenshot or frame of real output: our real
   reels, our real QA reports, our real ledger. No claim
   without a frame.
4. **Consistent visual identity for the pack.** Our
   cream / teal / ink system, not generic. Identity makes
   a list feel like a product.
5. **Rewrite-in-own-voice as the stated rule.** Our
   learnings area already enforces this. When the pack
   teaches studying others, it states the line explicitly:
   steal the mechanism, never the material.
