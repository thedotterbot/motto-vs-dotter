## What it is

A talking-head coaching reel: a numbered 3-step story
formula (problem → pursuit → payoff) presented as the engine
behind big launches, with an application beat and a
long-game beat. No B-roll, no screen recording, no
demonstration — just the speaker, two locations, and graphics.

## What works and why (the content, not the funnel)

- **Title-card number as hook.** Full-screen card: revenue
  figure + 17-month timeframe, over the speaker on a
  high-rise balcony. The number buys the first seconds; the
  balcony shot sells the lifestyle the number implies.
  Attention first, explanation second.
- **Numbered framework = feeling of a system.** A 3-step
  formula with named stages, presented one step at a time,
  each circled/highlighted as it is explained. Numbering
  makes advice feel complete and learnable, even when the
  steps are generic. The application beat (a three-part
  question format: struggle → overcome → result) shows the
  formula working, which is more convincing than the formula
  alone.
- **Cuts timed to topic shifts.** One continuous-feeling take
  alternating two setups (indoor table close-up / balcony
  medium). A new framework step = new graphic + shot change.
  The cut lands ON the idea change, not on a timer. That is
  the pacing rule worth stealing.
- **Word-by-word captions as the dominant motion.** Large
  bold white captions, key figures and step numbers given
  oversized graphic treatment. In a two-location talking-head
  reel, the captions ARE the motion.
- **Long-game beat as the closer.** Leaning on the railing,
  payoff compounds over time, planting-seeds idea. Ends on
  patience, which flatters the viewer who sticks around.

## What does NOT transfer to us

- **Proof: none.** No screenshots, dashboards, receipts,
  documents, testimonials, or before/after evidence in the
  observed frames. The only on-screen evidence is the
  title-card figure itself — the speaker's own claim. All
  proof asserted, not demonstrated. Same caution as the
  jacob entry: works for him, fails our rule.
- **Authority delivery.** Fast, confident, high-energy
  coaching down the lens. It works because the speaker
  performs certainty. Motto performing certainty would read
  as false — our character's authority comes from survived
  disasters, not claimed wins.
- **Two-location variety is a real-person luxury.** A
  talking head can walk to a balcony. A cutout puppet cannot.
  Our variety has to come from camera, props, and set
  changes, not locations.

## What we apply

1. **Cut on the idea change.** New beat = new shot/graphic/
   camera move, timed to the topic shift. Add to the Eye
   hat: verify cuts land on beat changes, not on a metronome.
2. **Numbered steps with named stages.** When a script teaches
   a process, name the stages and present them one at a time
   with a visual marker each. The feeling of a system is a
   retention device — use it for real processes (our pipeline),
   never for invented ones.
3. **Title-card number.** A concrete figure + timeframe in
   the first frame. Our hook chip can carry this: one number,
   one timeframe, no sentence.
