## What it is

A creator explains how she grew a brand-new account to 1.5M
views in 12 days with an AI character: "Rose," an AI grandma
posting wellness tips. The creator talks to camera; the
character appears only in screen-recorded stills and a
profile grid — never as an animated speaking character.

## What works and why (the content, not the funnel)

- **Named character + concrete niche + human origin.** Rose.
  Wellness tips. Inspired by the creator's mom and the tips
  she grew up with. Three facts and the character is real.
  This is the character formula in its simplest form: name,
  niche, origin. Motto has all three (name, learning to make
  videos, built by Muse) — the formula is sound; execution is
  the gap.
- **Proof inserts, not proof claims.** Two screen recordings
  break up the talking head: (1) Rose's profile — avatar,
  grid of AI older-woman photos, 27 posts, ~14.7K followers
  at recording time; (2) an insights overlay — ~313K views
  with four-figure likes/comments/shares/saves. The growth
  claim arrives WITH visible evidence. Contrast with the
  jacob and jun_yuh entries (proof: none). This is why this
  reel is believable and those read as hype.
- **Origin story as the structure.** Built-from-scratch →
  posted on a new account → numbers → how. The story does
  the teaching: the viewer learns the method by following
  the timeline, not by sitting through a framework.
- **Captions as the motion.** Mostly one continuous
  talking-head take, no rapid cuts. Attention is carried by
  captions changing every 1–2 seconds plus the two proof
  inserts. Same lesson as katecore: when the content has
  genuine beats, caption rhythm + inserts can carry pacing
  without jump cuts.
- **The character never performs — and it still works.**
  Rose doesn't speak, move, or animate. She exists in
  stills. The CREATOR does all the performing. A character
  can grow on stills + a human narrator. That is a production
  shortcut with a lesson for us: our bar (Motto must perform)
  is self-imposed and harder. If a still-image character can
  pull 1.5M views, our animated one has no excuse — the gap
  is jokes and loops, not render quality.

## What does NOT transfer to us

- **Human narrator.** We don't have one. Motto is the
  narrator AND the character. We can't split the job the way
  she does (creator talks, character poses). Our scripts
  have to make one plush do both.
- **Caption numbers vs video numbers.** The page caption
  lists larger current follower counts as text claims, not
  on-video proof. Only the recorded inserts count as shown
  evidence. Same discipline for us: report numbers with
  screenshots, never as caption text alone.
- **Wellness niche is inherently shareable.** Grandma +
  wellness tips = warm, sendable. Our niche (a plush
  learning video) is funny-first, not warm-first. Different
  share trigger; don't copy the tone.

## What we apply

1. **Proof inserts for growth updates.** When we report
   results (Scoreboard Sunday, milestones), SHOW the profile
   + insights as screen recordings, never claim numbers in
   caption text alone. Add to the tracking ritual: every
   number we publish gets a screenshot.
2. **Character formula check.** Every reel should re-earn
   name + niche + origin for a cold viewer in the first
   seconds. Our theme bit does this — audit it against
   Rose's three facts: is Motto's name, niche, and origin
   all legible by 10 seconds?
3. **Caption rhythm + inserts as pacing.** When a reel has
   real beats (proof, before/after, disaster), caption
   changes + inserts can carry pacing without jump cuts.
   Don't force fast cuts on a reel whose beats already move.
