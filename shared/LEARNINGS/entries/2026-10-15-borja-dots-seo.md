## What it is

A demo walkthrough: OpenAI's Dots agents (real — launched at
DevDay Sept 29, 2026, per multiple secondary sources) applied to
SEO backlinks. Talking-head hook, then almost entirely screen
recording: create a Dot, build a workflow, install an SEO plugin,
run a publish request, show the published output.

## What works and why (the content, not the funnel)

- **Hook = news + specific use case in 2 seconds.** Opening
  frame: talking head + a title card naming the payoff, first
  caption framing it as a just-launched feature, then an
  immediate pivot to one concrete application. Newness +
  application, no preamble.
- **The demo IS the content.** Every beat shows a click path
  with an output. No talking about the thing; showing the
  thing. The walkthrough functions as proof-by-process: the
  viewer watches each step produce something.
- **Specificity as credibility.** Named products, named
  features, concrete UI values (authority-style scores in a
  table, a weekly cadence, specific site names in a
  distribution list). This is the specificity principle in
  action — and it is what makes an AI-news reel feel verified
  even when the viewer cannot check any of it.
- **No static frames.** Cuts every few seconds, plus constant
  motion inside the screen recordings (scrolling, zooming,
  cursor movement). There is almost no moment where nothing
  moves. Contrast with our reels 01/02: static compositions
  were the recurring complaint.
- **Karaoke captions.** Large bold white uppercase captions,
  phrase-by-phrase, current word highlighted. The eye keeps
  moving even during the talking-head bookends.
- **Persistent banner.** A top banner runs across most of the
  video, so the offer stays visible without interrupting the
  demo.

## What does NOT transfer to us

- **Process shown, outcome not proven.** What is visible: the
  plugin page, install toggles, a "working" state, a draft
  preview, a distribution table, a published preview. What is
  NOT visible: any analytics dashboard, traffic, revenue, or
  before/after ranking proof. The reel works whether or not
  the workflow delivers results — the viewer cannot tell from
  the video. We do not show process that doesn't produce the
  outcome shown. Our disasters are honest process; this is
  process as costume.
- **The specific workflow claims are unverified.** The base
  product (Dots) is real. The plugin + backlink mechanics in
  the demo were not independently verified. Do not repeat them
  as fact.
- **We have no screen to record.** Character content cannot
  do a screen walkthrough. The transferable part is the
  structure (show, don't tell), not the format.

## What we apply

1. **Title card in the first 2 seconds that names the payoff.**
   Our hook chip already does this — keep it, and judge every
   hook by whether the payoff is legible before audio matters.
2. **Motion inside every frame.** The screen-recording
   equivalent for us: camera moves + prop motion + Motto
   motion. Never a static hold. This reinforces our >=2
   camera moves rule — treat a still frame as a defect.
3. **Show-the-process as proof.** Our version: show the
   disaster, not a claim about it. Reel 04's nothing beat is
   this principle in character form.
4. **Word-highlighted captions.** Our kinetic captions do this
   in part — keep the highlight-on-current-word behavior in
   talking-head-style beats.
