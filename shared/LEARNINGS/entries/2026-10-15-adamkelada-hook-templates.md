## What works and why (the content, not the funnel)

- **Example first, host second.** Opens on an example
  clip, THEN cuts to the host. Proof-of-concept before
  the teacher. Same output-first lesson as the
  ashokreddyai entry.
- **Numbered list as structure.** Countable,
  completable — same completion-loop as the
  kienobifilms entry (countable + template-consistent
  = completion loop).
- **One example clip per category.** Example per
  category — third occurrence in the set
  (kallawaymarketing, then this). A bare list is
  advice; a list with one concrete example per point
  is content.
- **Large labels as takeaways.** Gold/white hook
  labels readable in a paused frame. The label IS the
  point, not decoration. Same as the jun_yuh
  split-screen lesson: labels as premise.
- **Specific niche scenarios, not abstract advice.**
  The examples are concrete (outdoor night clip,
  supplement clip). Specificity again.
- **View-count badges make claims concrete in one
  glance.** A badge over a clip reads instantly — but
  see below.

## What does NOT transfer to us

- **The hook taxonomy is HIS framework.** Seven types
  (Expertise, Warning, List, Desire, Promise + two
  uncaptured). NOT adopted — same as the
  kallawaymarketing six-type taxonomy. Our hook work
  stays: cold-open hook + bumper + theme bit. Hook
  types are tools, not a taxonomy to copy. And hooks
  3–4 were not even captured — the taxonomy is
  incomplete in observation.
- **View-count badges are unverified graphics, not
  evidence.** A badge overlaid on a clip is a claim in
  a graphic's clothing — same as the devinjatho
  caution (numbers as-displayed graphics, not
  independently verified). Our rule: numbers on screen
  need a receipt (tracking sheet) or must be visibly
  a joke prop.
- **No joke, no gag.** Shareability from utility +
  badges. Not our engine (comedy + send test).
- **Template-offer close not verified in speech.**
  CTA not analyzed.

## What we apply

1. **Example first, host second.** Open on the
   proof-of-concept clip, then explain. Our
   deconstruction reels: show the disaster frame
   first, then Motto explains.
2. **One example per category.** Third occurrence.
   When we teach (pack content, teaching reels), one
   concrete example per point, never a bare list.
3. **Labels as takeaways.** Large on-screen labels
   readable in a paused frame. Our captions already
   do this; the lesson is the label IS the point, not
   decoration.
4. **Numbered structure as completion loop.**
   Countable = completable. Our carousels already use
   this; bring it to teaching reels.
5. **Badge skepticism.** View-count badges are
   graphics, not evidence. Panel Accountant check: any
   number on screen needs a receipt or a joke-prop
   framing.
