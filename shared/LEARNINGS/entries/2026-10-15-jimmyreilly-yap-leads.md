## What it is

A creator breaks down a prior reel — a casual-style
("yap") reel written by Claude — that pulled 13,400 leads.
Hook, then a full-screen Insights screenshot, then a
two-part framework: niche problem discovery + fast-solution
lead magnet.

## What works and why (the content, not the funnel)

- **Proof for a prior thing, labeled honestly.** The
  Insights screenshot shows the EARLIER reel's numbers:
  215,539 views, 154,222 accounts reached, 13.4K comments,
  3.9K shares, 14.3K saves, 5.4K likes. The video is clear
  the proof belongs to the earlier reel. That honesty is
  why it lands — the viewer trusts the numbers because
  they're scoped.
- **Read the ratios, not just the totals.** Comments
  (13.4K) dwarf likes (5.4K) — the comment-funnel
  fingerprint. But saves (14.3K) beat comments: people
  KEPT it. Saves are the signal the content was genuinely
  useful, not just funnel traffic. Validates our tracking
  choice: saves + shares per 1K reach as the decision
  metric, not likes.
- **Overlays as attention resets without cuts.** No hard
  camera cuts in the observed frames — one continuous
  talking-head take. Visual change comes from full-screen
  overlays (title cards, Insights screenshot) popping
  in and out over the speaker. Each overlay resets
  attention without cutting the performance. This
  validates our chip/tag grammar: overlay cards popping
  over Motto do the same job.
- **Framework with named parts.** Two named stages,
  each with its own title card. Naming makes a generic
  idea memorable: one problem for one audience, paired
  with a quick-solution offer. The name is the mnemonic.
- **Specificity everywhere.** Exact lead count in the hook
  title, exact Insights figures, named framework parts.
  No vague advice anywhere in the observed beats.

## What does NOT transfer to us

- **Human talking head.** We don't have one. Our
  equivalent of "overlays over a continuous take" is
  overlay cards over Motto's hold — same mechanic, puppet
  version. The principle transfers; the shot doesn't.
- **The framework IS the funnel.** Niche problem +
  fast-solution magnet is a funnel design, not just
  content. We already run this shape (PACK funnel). The
  learning here is the PRESENTATION (proof first, named
  parts, overlays), not the funnel itself.
- **"Claude wrote it" is the novelty claim.** For him,
  AI-written is the hook. For us, AI-made is the premise
  of every video — it can't also be the hook. Our hook has
  to be the joke or the disaster, never "an AI made this."

## What we apply

1. **Overlays reset attention without cuts.** Title cards,
   chips, tags, and screenshots popping over Motto's hold.
   Add to the Eye hat: every 5–8 seconds without a cut
   needs an overlay event, or the frame is dead.
2. **Label what proof is proof OF.** When we show numbers,
   say which reel/post they belong to, on screen. Never let
   a screenshot imply it proves more than it does.
3. **Name our frameworks tightly.** Our panel checks, our
   QA gates, our production loop — give each a short name
   and a title card when we teach them. Named = memorable.
4. **Read saves in our own tracking.** Saves beating
   comments = genuinely useful. Comments beating saves +
   likes = funnel traffic. Judge each reel by which pattern
   it shows.
