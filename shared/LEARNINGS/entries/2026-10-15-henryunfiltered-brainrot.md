## What it is

Irreverent adult parody / brainrot-style comedy. Pig-like
characters in adult domestic conflict, rendered as
children's-show-style AI animation. Opening frame: two
pig-like characters in bed with a phone on a tripod
recording them, with a single-word startled caption.

Structure (observed order, sampled across loops): bedroom
setup (startled / arguing reaction) → diner cutaway (a
donkey/horse-like server character behind a counter, in
an exchange about being labeled a pig) → bedroom return
(one pig character in pajamas distressed / crying while
the other comforts / speaks closely) → hallway
confrontation (an older pig character in plaid shirt, cap
and glasses points accusingly at a towel-clad pig in a
doorway, with a mother-like pig figure watching behind) →
return to bedroom, loop repeats. Rapid cuts between
bedroom, diner, and hallway. Large all-caps captions
synced to dialogue. No lull, short confrontational beats.

## What works and why (the content, not the funnel)

- **Visual premise in frame 1.** Pigs in bed + phone on
  tripod = the whole setup readable without a word. No
  explanation needed. The tripod phone IS the premise
  (caught on camera).
- **Shock contrast engine.** Cute, familiar character
  designs + adult domestic conflict (melodramatic
  arguments, exposure premise). Contrast IS the joke —
  same mismatch engine as the emergentlabs entry (cute +
  epic). The mismatch does the work; the dialogue just
  rides it.
- **Location changes as pacing.** Three locations in one
  short reel (bedroom / diner / hallway), short
  confrontational beats, no lull. Location changes are
  the pacing device — the Eye rule in reverse: cuts do
  what overlays would.
- **Large all-caps captions synced to dialogue.**
  Readable sound-off. Caption layer carries the beat
  even muted.
- **Reaction scale.** Exaggerated facial expressions
  and gestures carry scenes. Same lesson as the
  kellyxgallo and catbaby entries: reactions are the
  content.
- **Engagement shape as signal.** 8.2K likes vs 93
  comments — likes far exceed comments. Entertainment
  posts look like this; funnel posts look the opposite
  (comments exceed likes). Engagement shape tells you
  the mechanism.

## What does NOT transfer to us

- **Borrowed children's-show-style characters in
  adult-coded conflict = IP risk + platform risk.** We
  do NOT put recognizable children's characters in adult
  situations. Our characters are our own (Motto, Dotter).
  The mechanism (contrast) transfers; the material does
  not.
- **Misclassification danger.** Cute / child-coded
  designs in adult-coded content risk the worst possible
  platform misread. Stay far from that line: our edgy =
  innuendo between adult-coded original characters,
  never child-coded designs.
- **Proof: none.** Entirely fictional AI animation. Fine
  for comedy, but nothing here teaches proof.
- **Dialogue unverified.** Muted player, captions sampled
  as still frames. Exact wording not captured.

## What we apply

1. **Visual premise in frame 1.** Panel check: can a
   cold viewer state the premise from the first frame
   alone? The tripod phone is the test — one prop that
   IS the setup.
2. **Contrast engine, own characters.** Cute plush +
   adult-coded situation (Motto's disasters framed as
   melodrama). Contrast is the joke; our characters
   carry it.
3. **Location changes as pacing.** Our set can redress
   (desk → floor → close-up) as location changes. Three
   "locations" in one short reel, no lull.
4. **Engagement shape tells the mechanism.** When we
   read our own metrics: likes >> comments = joke
   landed; comments >> likes = funnel working.
5. **Reaction scale.** Exaggerated reactions carry
   scenes without dialogue. Keep pushing Motto's
   reactions bigger.
