## What it is

Directly our territory: videos made from code, no editing
app. Beats (sampled from a looping player): hook —
direct-to-camera speaker in split screen with code,
captions "You can now" / "entirely from code," plus a "NO
EDITING APP" title card → contrast / pain point: a paid
video-tool pricing/credits screen crossed out, a
watermarked clip, a "no credits" message — framing the
method as free with no credits or watermark → layered
breakdown: numbered stacked-timeline graphics (captured:
Layer 03/05 Captions, Layer 04/05 Transitions, Layer 05/05
Sound FX; Layers 01–02 not captured) → proof example: a
generated documentary-style clip (ancient-city eruption
scene) shown full-screen and split-screen beside code →
ending / CTA not verified in sampling.

## What works and why (the content, not the funnel)

- **Split-screen code + output is the strongest proof
  form for code-made video.** Cause and effect in one
  frame: the code on one side, its result on the other.
  You do not have to trust that code made the clip — you
  watch them together. This is the demo grammar our
  pipeline content should use.
- **Contrast makes the alternative concrete.** A crossed-
  out pricing screen, a watermarked clip, a "no credits"
  message. The alternative is SHOWN, not named. Concrete
  contrast beats abstract claims ("better," "cheaper").
- **Numbered layers promise a complete system.**
  "Layer 03/05" tells you there is a full stack. The
  numbering itself promises completeness — a system, not
  a trick. Same mechanism as opusjake's bundle arc.
- **Serious-output proof.** The proof clip is a
  documentary-style eruption scene, not a toy demo. A
  serious-looking output proves the method handles real
  content. Toy demos prove the tool runs; serious outputs
  prove it matters.
- **Constraint framed as identity.** "NO EDITING APP" —
  a constraint worn as a feature. Same positioning as our
  "made on free Muse": the limitation becomes the brand.
- **Three-mode rotation.** Speaker + code + generated
  clips, fast cuts between them. The mode change is the
  pacing — same three-mode variety as the last two
  entries.

## What does NOT transfer to us

- **Proof is demo-only.** No metrics, no before/after,
  no testimonials. The eruption clip is shown, but the
  reel does not show the code that made THAT clip, nor
  the iteration behind it. A finished clip beside code
  is suggestive, not conclusive.
- **"Free, no credits, no watermark" needs scrutiny.**
  Our honesty rule: free has a cap (our free Muse usage
  has a weekly limit, already 45% used). A "no credits"
  claim that hides a cap would fail our Accountant test.
  If we ever make a "no credits" claim, the cap gets
  disclosed in the same breath.
- **The 5-layer stack is HIS framework.** Layers 01–02
  were not even captured — do not present his system as
  understood. We do not copy his layer stack; our pipeline
  has its own stages (script → voice → compose → QA →
  gate-8).
- **Price framing stays dead.** The crossed-out pricing
  screen works as contrast — but our L-09 rule stands:
  never the "$60 vs free" price-vs-value framing, which
  flatters the rival. Contrast on constraints and freedom,
  not on price.
- **Talking-head delivery is not our format.** Our bet is
  character-led. The explainer energy works here; it does
  not transfer to a plush puppet's delivery.
- **Ending / CTA not verified.** Do not describe a close
  that was not observed.

## What we apply

1. **Split-screen code + output as our demo grammar.**
   When we demo the pipeline, show the HyperFrames
   composition / code beside the rendered frame in ONE
   frame. Cause + effect together.
2. **Show the alternative concretely.** When positioning
   against paid tools, show the alternative (crossed-out
   pricing, watermark) rather than naming it — without
   the price-vs-value framing (L-09).
3. **Number our pipeline stages.** Present our stages as
   a numbered system (script → voice → compose → QA →
   gate-8) so the viewer sees a system, not a trick.
4. **Serious-output proof.** Our proof clips are real
   reels (01 / 02 / 04), not toy demos. Real output is
   the only proof that counts.
5. **Constraint as identity, cap disclosed.** "Made on
   free Muse" stated the way "NO EDITING APP" is stated
   — a constraint worn as identity, with the weekly cap
   disclosed honestly in the same breath.
